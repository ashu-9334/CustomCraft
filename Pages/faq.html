Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about CustomCraft.

What is CustomCraft?

CustomCraft is a personalized shopping platform where you can customize products and create meaningful gifts.

Can I customize a T-Shirt?

Yes. You can choose the T-Shirt color, size, and add your own text.

Can I create a Gift Box?

Yes. You can select a box type, choose a gift item, and add a personal message.

How can I track my order?

After placing an order, you receive an Order ID. Use the Track Order page to check your order status.

Can I add products to Wishlist?

Yes. Click the heart button on a product to save it to your Wishlist.

How can I contact CustomCraft?

Visit the Contact Us page and send us your message.
